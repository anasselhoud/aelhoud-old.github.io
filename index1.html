About

French-Moroccan, Doctor in AI & Computer Science, Laureate of Centrale Lyon & Arts et Métiers with double engineering degrees.

Currently a Digital Strategy Manager at FORVIA, leading digital transformation projects, Data, AI & GenAI Roadmap at Global group level.

My areas of expertise include industrial AI, data science, digital transformation strategy and management. I am deeply invested in the innovation, development, implementation, and integration of AI within various industries while building global digital strategies.

As a tech enthusiast, I enjoy experimenting with single-board computers and microcontrollers like Raspberry Pi and Arduino, creating open-source AI models, and conducting penetration testing.
