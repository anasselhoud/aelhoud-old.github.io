About

PhD, AI & Data - Tech Lead & Manager.

  • Location: France
  • Collaboration: Available
  • Degree: PhD, Double Eng.

Laureate of Centrale Lyon & Arts et Métiers with double Engineer's degree (~ 2 x MSc) and currently leading and pursing a CIFRE PhD thesis with FORVIA and FEMTO-ST. My areas of expertise include artificial intelligence, data science, digital transformation and strategy, and digital project management. I'm particularly interested in leading and managing the development, deployment, and integration of AI in industry. I believe that AI has the potential to revolutionize the way we work, and I'm excited to be a part of the digital transformation that is currently underway in many industries. As a tech passionate, I also enjoy playing with single-board computers/microcontrollers, like Raspberry Pi and Arduino, as well as pentesting devices and tools.
